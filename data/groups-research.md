# Marine research & conservation groups — Rio ↔ Ilhabela corridor

**Window:** ~2023–2026  
**Updated:** 2026-09-18 (America/Sao_Paulo)  
**Method:** Public news, scientific papers, institutional reports, and program outcome pages. Ranked by documented positive impact and activity density in the corridor (Costa Verde, Litoral Norte SP, Cabo Frio / Lagos where relevant). Prefer measured outcomes over marketing. Uncertainty noted where claims are self-reported or attribution is shared across partners.

**Geographic note:** Corridor treated as Angra/Paraty/Ilha Grande → Litoral Norte SP (Ubatuba–Ilhabela), with Cabo Frio / Arraial / Búzios and Rio coastal islands included when activity clearly touches the same migratory or management systems.

## Ranked groups

### 1. Instituto Argonauta para a Conservação Costeira e Marinha
- **Website:** https://institutoargonauta.org/
- **Focus:** Beach/fauna monitoring (PMP-BS), rescue & rehab (CRETA), marine mammal sightings (AMMA), marine litter monitoring & education
- **Region:** Litoral Norte SP — Ubatuba, Caraguatatuba, São Sebastião, Ilhabela
- **Evidence:**
  - **2023-06-27** — Team disentangled a humpback whale (~8–9 m) from longline gear off Ilhabela (ICMBio protocols). https://noticiasdaspraias.com/2023/06/27/instituto-argonauta-libera-baleia-jubarte-presa-em-cabo-de-pesca-em-ilhabela/
  - **Ongoing through 2025** — Executes PMP-BS Trecho 10: ~218 beaches / ~114 km daily monitoring; CRETA rehab (Ubatuba) + stabilization (São Sebastião). https://institutoargonauta.org/new/projetos/projeto-de-monitoramento-de-praiaspmp-bs/
  - **Bulletins archived through Dec 2025** — Monthly *Boletim do Lixo* on Litoral Norte beaches. https://institutoargonauta.org/new/boletim-do-lixo/
  - **Self-reported through Jul/2025** — Org cites ~47 t beach litter removed (2016–2025) and >19k wildlife occurrences; treat cumulative totals as institutional claims. https://institutoargonauta.org/new/quem-somos/
- **Score note:** Highest continuous operational footprint on the SP side (daily beaches + rehab + litter + mammal work).

### 2. Fundação Projeto Tamar — base Ubatuba
- **Website:** https://www.tamar.org.br/ · Ubatuba center: https://www.tamar.org.br/centros_visitantes.php?cod=9
- **Focus:** Green turtle feeding-area monitoring, incidental-catch mitigation with floating weirs (*cercos flutuantes*), rehab/release, education museum
- **Region:** Ubatuba (feeding area); rehab receives animals from Ilhabela / Litoral Norte via PMP partners
- **Evidence:**
  - **2024 activity report** — Ubatuba rehab; floating-weir monitoring with tagging and return to sea; museum education. http://tamar.org.br/transparencia/Relatorio_Atividades_Fundacao_Projeto_Tamar_2024_web.pdf
  - **2024-10 → 2025-07** — 128 turtle captures recorded and returned at Camburi floating weirs (~1.2/day). https://www.tamar.org.br/noticia1.php?cod=1086
  - **2025 activity report** — Camburi weir monitoring continues (report cites 118 green turtles in its 2025 framing). http://tamar.org.br/transparencia/Relatorio_Atividades_Fundacao_Projeto_Tamar_2025.pdf
- **Score note:** Long time series + measurable bycatch returns. National org with a concrete local base (feeding/rehab, not nesting).

### 3. Projeto Baleia Jubarte (Instituto Baleia Jubarte)
- **Website:** https://www.baleiajubarte.org.br/principal
- **Focus:** Humpback research (photo-ID, biopsies, photogrammetry), SE migration corridor science, responsible whale-watching capacity building
- **Region:** Expeditions covering Paraty → Arraial do Cabo (broader Brazilian range centered on Abrolhos)
- **Evidence:**
  - **2024-06/07 → published 2024-08-05** — *Expedição Sudeste 2024*: 26 cruises, ~135 sea hours, 775 nm, **223 humpbacks** (1 calf), 124 photo-IDs, 5 biopsies, 25 photogrammetry sets; also southern right whale mother-calf. https://www.baleiajubarte.org.br/post/expedi%C3%A7%C3%A3o-sudeste-2024-movimenta-cen%C3%A1rio-da-pesquisa-e-turismo-na-regi%C3%A3o
  - **2024-07** — Third consecutive Arraial visit; community-based tourism boarding for sustainable cetacean watching. https://odia.ig.com.br/arraial-do-cabo/2024/07/6880391-projeto-baleia-jubarte-afirma-que-arraial-e-o-local-mais-acessivel-no-brasil-para-avistar-baleias.html
  - **2024-07** — Folha coverage of population recovery narrative and RJ calf sightings tied to project researchers. https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/ambiente/2024/07/aparicao-de-baleias-jubarte-no-brasil-cresceu-30-vezes-em-30-anos-dizem-pesquisadores-veja-fotos.shtml
- **Score note:** Strong corridor-scale science and tourism-regulation influence. Core base Bahia/ES; SE work seasonal but well documented for 2024.

### 4. Instituto PROSHARK
- **Website:** https://www.proshark.org.br/
- **Focus:** Shark & ray conservation science — satellite/acoustic telemetry, drones, citizen sightings; Ilha Grande Bay elasmobranch refuge
- **Region:** Angra dos Reis / Baía da Ilha Grande (Piraquara)
- **Evidence:**
  - **Founded 2024** — Angra-based institute; integrated monitoring described on org site. https://www.proshark.org.br/
  - **2025 paper (BJAER)** — Blacktip aggregation observations and initial multi-species satellite tracking in Ilha Grande Bay (tags 2022–2024). https://doi.org/10.34188/bjaerv8n1-124
  - **2025–2026 paper trail** — Tiger shark aggregation / pioneer satellite telemetry in SE Brazil; paper cites PROSHARK supplying shark/ray data for MPA discussions. https://doi.org/10.34188/bjaerv9n1-128
  - **2025** — Piraquara de Fora designated IUCN Important Shark and Ray Area (ISRA); org and press link local monitoring to the listing (attribution shared with Mar Urbano / Tubarões da BIG). Org site also claims Nature/Science pubs by associates and Fantástico coverage (Jan 2025) — **not independently verified here**.
- **Score note:** High scientific leverage for a young NGO. Overlaps with IBRACON/Mar Urbano shark work; treat as shared research ecosystem.

### 6. Instituto Albatroz — PMP Região dos Lagos
- **Website:** https://projetoalbatroz.org.br/
- **Focus:** Daily beach monitoring, rescue/rehab of seabirds & turtles (PMP-BC/ES licensing condition), necropsy, education
- **Region:** Cabo Frio, Búzios, part of Arraial do Cabo (~54 km / 25 beaches); CRD in Araruama
- **Evidence:**
  - **2024-06 start** — Albatroz begins PMP-BC/ES: daily foot/ATV patrols; first month 48 animals (17 live / 31 dead). https://projetoalbatroz.org.br/sobre-o-projeto-albatroz/noticias/instituto-albatroz-da-inicio-a-execucao-do-projeto-de-monitoramento-de-praias-pmp-na-regiao-dos-lagos
  - **2024-06-10 → 2025-06** — One-year tally: **216 live + 353 dead** finds; **55 rehabilitated releases**. https://portalfluminense.com.br/2025/06/16/instituto-albatroz-completa-um-ano-de-resgates-e-monitoramento-na-regiao-dos-lagos/
- **Score note:** Clear quantitative year-one outcomes. Licensing-funded, but outcomes are real and public. Lagos is east of Costa Verde but in-scope per brief.

### 7. Programa Mar Sem Lixo (Fundação Florestal / Semil-SP)

## Ranking uncertainty

- **Shark cluster (#4–#5):** PROSHARK, Mar Urbano, and IBRACON/Tubarões da BIG operate in the same bay with overlapping narratives (aggregations, ISRA). Papers vs press credits differ. Do not treat any single org as sole owner of the Piraquara story.
- **Licensing programs (PMP, Mar Sem Lixo, some Petrobras Socioambiental projects):** High activity because of legal/funding mandates. Outcomes are real; “impact” includes compliance-driven scale.
- **Self-reported website totals** (Argonauta cumulative litter/fauna; PROSHARK journal prestige claims): Prefer dated news, DOIs, and government tallies when ranking.
